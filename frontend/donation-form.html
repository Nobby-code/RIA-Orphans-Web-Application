How to donate
  1. Open the M-Pesa menu on your phone.
  2. Select Lipa na M-Pesa, then Pay Bill.
  3. Enter the Paybill number shown in the image above.
  4. Enter the foundation's account number shown in the image.
  5. Enter your donation amount and confirm the details.
  6. Enter your M-Pesa PIN to complete the transaction.
Please confirm the Paybill and account details before completing your payment.
